# Dusrel branding rules

Yeh rules **saare Dusrel products** par lagu hote hain — web apps, Android, extension, aur dusrel.com khud.

## Golden rule: text-only co-branding

- Product ka **apna logo hamesha untouched** rehta hai
- Dusrel ka sparkle **kabhi product UI mein nahi** aata
- Sirf text se co-branding: `by Dusrel`, `A Dusrel product`

> Yeh faisla liya ja chuka hai — har product mein same pattern. Naya product banao toh yahi follow karo.

## Pattern (har product mein same)

| Jagah | Kya likhna hai |
|---|---|
| Header | Logo/wordmark ke neeche chhota `by Dusrel` micro-text |
| Footer | Tagline ke neeche `A Dusrel product` → link `https://dusrel.com` |
| About page | Ek paragraph: "X is a Dusrel product — part of a privacy-first family of free online tools…" |
| README.md | Aakhir mein: `> A **Dusrel** product — part of the [Dusrel](https://dusrel.com) ecosystem.` |
| Manifest (extension) | Name: `ShrinkIt — Image Resizer by Dusrel` |

## dusrel.com par

dusrel.com khud Dusrel ka ghar hai — yahan full branding hai: sparkle logo, "Dusrel" wordmark, ecosystem grid. Yahan co-branding ki zaroorat nahi kyunki yeh main brand hai.

## Kya NAHI karna

- ❌ Product logo ko Dusrel sparkle se replace karna
- ❌ Product ke UI mein sparkle ghusana
- ❌ "by Dusrel" ko itna bada karna ki product ka naam dab jaye — micro-text hi rehna chahiye
- ❌ Footer link par `target="_blank"` + `rel="noopener"` bhoolna
